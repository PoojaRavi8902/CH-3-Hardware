3.2.1 Advanced Input Devices

Input devices convert real-world data into a digital format that the computer can process.

Barcode Readers vs. QR Codes

Feature Barcode (1D) QR Code (2D)
Data Capacity Small (usually just an ID number) Large (can store URLs, Wi-Fi keys, or text)
Technology Laser or Red LED measures reflection Imager (camera) processes pixel patterns

Digital Cameras & Scanners

2D Scanners: Use a bright light and a CCD (Charge-Coupled Device) to convert reflected light into electric signals (binary).
3D Scanners: Use lasers or X-rays to measure the dimensions (x, y, z coordinates) of a solid object to create a digital CAD model.

3.2.2 Sensors & Control Systems

In the IGCSE syllabus, sensors are used in **Monitoring** (observing data) and **Control** (taking action via actuators).

The Control Loop Step-by-Step:

  1. Sensor sends Analogue signals.
  2. The ADC (Analogue to Digital Converter) converts it for the microprocessor.
  3. Microprocessor compares data to a Pre-set Value.
  4. If data is outside the range, it sends a signal to the Actuator (via a DAC).

Common Sensors Encyclopedia

Sensor How it Works (Physical Change) Application
Temperature Resistance changes as heat fluctuates. Ovens, Greenhouses, AC units.
Infrared (Motion) Detects changes in heat radiation from objects. Intruder alarms, automatic doors.
Acoustic Detects sound levels (decibels). Security systems, smart home voice control.
pH Sensor Measures acidity or alkalinity of a solution. Water treatment, chemical plants.

3.2.3 Output Devices & Actuators

Printers Comparison

Inkjet: Uses liquid ink cartridges and "Piezoelectric" or "Thermal Bubble" technology to spray droplets. Best for photos.

Laser: Uses a rotating drum, static electricity, and Toner (dry powder). A fuser unit melts the toner onto paper. Best for high-volume text.

DLP vs LCD Projectors

  • LCD: Uses three liquid crystal panels (RGB). Light passes through them to create the image.
  • DLP: Uses millions of tiny Micro-mirrors on a chip (DMD) that tilt towards or away from the light source.
