Global Hardware Review

The "Cheat Sheet" for your IGCSE Computer Science Exam.

1. The CPU "Traffic Control"

Think of the CPU not just as a brain, but as a factory floor. The Control Unit (CU) is the manager giving orders, while the ALU is the worker doing the heavy lifting.

The Register Shortcut

  • PC: The "To-Do" list (Next address).
  • MAR: The "Mailbox" (Address we are visiting now).
  • MDR: The "Package" (Actual data/instruction).
  • ACC: The "Workbench" (Where math happens).
Real-World Example:

If you are following a recipe:
- The Recipe Book is the RAM.
- The Line Number you're on is the PC.
- The Ingredients in your hand are the MDR.

2. Sensing vs. Acting

A microprocessor cannot "feel" the world without a sensor, and it cannot "change" the world without an actuator.

The "Pre-set" Rule:

The microprocessor never decides on its own. It constantly compares the Current Value (from ADC) to a Pre-set Value (stored in memory). If they don't match, it triggers the actuator.

3. Storage Comparison

In the exam, you often have to choose the best storage for a scenario. Use this comparison:

Scenario Best Choice Why?
Operating System Booting SSD High speed, no latency.
Long-term Server Backup Magnetic Tape Lowest cost per Terabyte.
Transferring small PDF USB Flash Portability / Offline storage.

4. MAC vs IP: The Final Distinction

MAC Address

Like your Fingerprint. It is unique to the physical hardware and cannot be changed.

Used for: Identification within a local network.

IP Address

Like your Mailing Address. It changes depending on which network you join.

Used for: Routing data across the Internet.
